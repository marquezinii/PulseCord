# Brand assets

`assets/pulsecord-logo.png` is the official PulseCord product mark supplied by the project owner. It is the single source of truth for PulseCord surfaces. The Windows and Linux/macOS package icons in `build/icon.ico` and `build/icon.png` are generated from this image; do not redraw or substitute it in individual features. The current approved source is the transparent PulseCord mark supplied on 28/07/2026.

`assets/discord-symbol.svg` is the unmodified white Discord symbol downloaded from Discord's official branding page. It is used only to identify the Discord service inside PulseCord and is not the PulseCord product logo.

The symbol, the Discord name, and related marks remain property of Discord Inc. They are excluded from PulseCord's GPL license. Do not recolor, distort, reconfigure, or redistribute the asset outside the uses allowed by Discord's current brand guidelines.

PulseCord is an unofficial project and is not created, sponsored, or endorsed by Discord Inc.

Inside Discord, PulseCord may overlay its product mark on the native Messages Direct/Home control without replacing the control or its behavior. The user-facing “Seguir Discord” preference removes that overlay and lets Discord render whichever in-app icon it currently permits. PulseCord does not alter, unlock, or redistribute Discord's Nitro app-icon choices.
